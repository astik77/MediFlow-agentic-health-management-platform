EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================
Phase 0 (Foundation), Phase 1 (Authentication & Patient Profile),
Phase 2 (Medical History Foundation), and Phase 3 (Secure Document
Management) are implemented and verified. Patients can securely upload,
verify (MIME + magic bytes + SHA-256), organize, search, filter, update,
download, and soft-archive medical documents in a patient-isolated vault
integrated with their chronological medical timeline and linked via FK to
structured health records.

Document intelligence / OCR pipelines, local AI inference, AI health
assistant, and RAG vector retrieval remain scheduled for Phases 4-7
(status: NOT YET APPLICABLE / NOT RUN).
